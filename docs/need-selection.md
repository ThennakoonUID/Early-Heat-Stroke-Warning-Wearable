# Need Selection

Five opportunities were investigated (need finding via clinician interviews), then scored 1–5 across five criteria (25 max) to choose the project focus.

| Need | Market | Patient Impact | Provider Impact | Feasibility | Team Preference | Total | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Heat stroke early warning wearable** | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | **20** | **1** |
| Joint angle / ROM tracker for physical therapy | 3 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 17 | 2 |
| Diabetic foot ulcer pressure/temp monitor | 3 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 15 | 3 |
| CKDu early symptom identification | 2 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 14 | 4 |
| Non-invasive dental caries detection (fluorescence) | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 12 | 5 |

**Selected: Opportunity 1 — Heat Stroke Early Warning Wearable**, based on strong market demand, high patient/provider impact, and strongest team preference, with acceptable technical feasibility for a semester-long undergraduate build.

Full write-up (need statements, disease fundamentals, existing solutions, stakeholder & market analysis for all 5 opportunities) is in `BDD_Investigation_Report.pdf` in this folder.
